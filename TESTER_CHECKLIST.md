# Freequiz tester checklist

Thanks for trying Freequiz. Please use the app without someone guiding you. The goal is to find confusing or broken behavior, not to rate the design.

**App:** https://oliverzsuffa.github.io/freequiz/

**Sample bank:** https://oliverzsuffa.github.io/freequiz/sample-question-bank.json

Use only the sample bank for this test. Do not share your own question banks, full backups, or personal learning history in feedback.

## 1. First use and importing

- [ ] Open Freequiz and find **More → Import / export**.
- [ ] Download the sample JSON file and select it in Freequiz.
- [ ] Choose **Validate and preview**. Check that the preview explains what will be added.
- [ ] Choose **Merge into profile** and confirm the sample questions appear.
- [ ] Could you complete this without help? If not, note where you got stuck.

## 2. Studying

- [ ] Go to Study and start practice.
- [ ] Answer a few questions and confirm you understand the feedback.
- [ ] Favourite a question.
- [ ] Defer a question. Confirm it skips the question without recording an answer.
- [ ] Start a timed exam, answer a question, and find the exit/submit controls.
- [ ] Submit the exam and confirm you can understand the result.

## 3. Filters, menus, and navigation

- [ ] Open More and confirm every menu item is reachable.
- [ ] Open tag search, then metadata filters and general question search.
- [ ] Try a filter, sorting, and Clear filters.
- [ ] Confirm the shown questions match the selected filters.
- [ ] Check that menus and pop-ups are not clipped or hidden behind the bottom navigation.
- [ ] On a phone, rotate to landscape and test a short viewport if possible.

## 4. Export and data safety

- [ ] Find **Export questions to share** and read what it includes.
- [ ] Find **Full profile backup** and **Backup all profiles**. Can you tell which is intended for sharing and which is private?
- [ ] Refresh the page and confirm your sample questions and progress remain.

Do not clear browser/site data during the test. You can inspect export options, but do not send full backup files to anyone.

## 5. Report any issue

For each problem, record:

- **Device and browser:** phone/desktop, browser name/version if known.
- **Screen size/orientation:** for visual issues.
- **Steps:** what you clicked, in order.
- **Expected:** what you thought should happen.
- **Actual:** what happened instead.
- **Repeatability:** every time, sometimes, or once.

Please report blockers first. Do not include private question banks, full backup JSON, passwords, or personal learning history.
